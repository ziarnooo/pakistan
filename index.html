Day by day

The itinerary, in full.

Each day balances driving, hiking and rest. Expect a balance of road journeys, rewarding hikes, local culture and moments to slow down. As in any mountain region, the itinerary may adapt to weather and road conditions.

0109.05 · Sun

Islamabad — arrival & Faisal Mosque

Arrive in Islamabad, where our team will meet you at the airport and transfer you to the hotel for breakfast and a short rest. After lunch, we’ll have time to exchange money before visiting the iconic Faisal Mosque and taking a relaxed stroll at Daman-e-Koh for sweeping views over the city. We’ll return to the hotel for dinner.

Islamabad
0210.05 · Mon

Skardu — flight north & Kharpocho Fort

We take an early flight north to Skardu, with spectacular mountain views if the weather allows. After lunch by the lake, we visit Kharpocho Fort, perched above town with views across the valley and the Indus River. Then we’ll settle in and enjoy our first evening in the mountains.

Skardu
0311.05 · Tue

Skardu — Marsur Rock & local life

A jeep ride takes us to the trailhead for Marsur Rock. The hike is challenging, but the panorama is worth it; stepping onto the rock for photos is entirely optional. After lunch, we’ll take some time to rest before heading into Skardu for a wander around the local market and dinner in town.

Marsur Rock
0412.05 · Wed

Fairy Meadows

We leave early for a scenic drive, followed by one of the region’s most thrilling off-road jeep routes. From the drop-off, we hike up to Fairy Meadows, stopping for lunch along the way. Sunset over Nanga Parbat, dinner and a bonfire close out the day.

Fairy Meadows
0513.05 · Thu

Nanga Parbat Base Camp

A long day hike to Nanga Parbat Base Camp, with lunch and mountain views along the way. The route includes some steeper sections and requires a good level of basic fitness. Back at Fairy Meadows, enjoy a well-earned dinner and chai.

Nanga Parbat
0614.05 · Fri

Karimabad — recovery in Hunza

We hike back down to the jeep point and continue by road to Karimabad. After checking into our accommodation, the afternoon is for rest. Karimabad is known for gemstones, jewellery, cafés and local handicrafts.

Karimabad
0715.05 · Sat

Ultar Meadows

For those who want an extra challenge, an optional hike takes us to Ultar Meadows, a viewpoint framed by cliffs and glaciers. The trail is steep in places. Back in Karimabad, enjoy a late lunch and free time for cafés, handicraft shops or a relaxed evening.

Ultar Meadows
0816.05 · Sun

Hunza & Eagle’s Nest

We’ll start the day exploring Baltit Fort, one of Hunza’s most iconic historic landmarks. In the afternoon, we’ll wander through the old settlement of Altit before travelling by vehicle to Eagle’s Nest for sunset over the dramatic Hunza Valley. Afterwards, we’ll return to Karimabad for dinner and another night at our accommodation.

Eagle’s Nest
0917.05 · Mon

Hapakun Camp

We drive to Minapin village for lunch, then begin the hike to Hapakun Camp through forest and open mountain scenery. We arrive in the afternoon, settle in and have dinner at camp.

Hapakun Camp
1018.05 · Tue

Rakaposhi Base Camp

Hike toward Rakaposhi Base Camp for close-up views of the glacier and surrounding peaks. After lunch in Minapin, we continue to our homestay in Ghulkin for a relaxed afternoon and dinner with our hosts.

Rakaposhi
1119.05 · Wed

Baskochi Meadows & Passu Cones

A steep, rewarding hike with views over Attabad Lake. After lunch, we continue to the iconic Passu Cones and explore the surrounding landscape, with time for the famous suspension bridge if conditions allow.

Passu Cones
1220.05 · Thu

Attabad Lake & the drive to Skardu

We’ll start the morning with a boat ride across the turquoise waters of Attabad Lake, formed by a landslide in 2010. Afterwards, we’ll set off on a full-day drive back to Skardu, travelling through the spectacular landscapes of northern Pakistan and preparing for our flight to Islamabad the following day.

Attabad Lake
1321.05 · Fri

Goodbyes in Skardu & flight to Islamabad

After breakfast, we’ll head to Skardu Airport, say our goodbyes and board our flight back to Islamabad. The expedition may be ending, but we hope you’ll be leaving with new friendships, unforgettable mountain memories and stories to share.
